Key Formulas

Snell's Law:

$$n_1 \sin(\theta_1) = n_2 \sin(\theta_2)$$

Critical Angle ($\theta_c$):

$$\sin(\theta_c) = \frac{n_2}{n_1} \implies \theta_c = \arcsin\left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right)$$

Total Internal Reflection (TIR) occurs when $\theta_1 > \theta_c$ and $n_1 > n_2$.
